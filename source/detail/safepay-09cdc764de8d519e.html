Revenue $7 Million

Incorporated in 1973, RUS Industries has steadily expanded its operations, including 100% growth over the last decade, to become a leading resource for general contract machine shop applications.

Housed in a 29,000 square foot, brick and metal construction facility, RUS Industries dedicates over 50% of its work space to machining operations. The two acre grounds reside in close proximity to key industrial resources, and allows more than adequate space for further expansions.

RUS Industries offers a quality-oriented management team that collaborates with our customers. Positioned for stable, low cost materials acquisition, RUS Industries management staff has instituted a complete system for the efficient planning, purchasing, scheduling, and order fulfillment of new and repeat orders. And as an “E-2” shop, materials, operations, order controls, revision number controls, and cost tracking are all carefully monitored and instantly accessible.

Throughout fulfillment, orders undergo a comprehensive quality control program. From initial requirements review and first article approval, through in-process inspection, documented operator in-process inspections, testing in an atmospherically-controlled quality lab, and documentation via the inspection oversight team, you can remain confident in the outcome and performance of your RUS Industries machined orders.
