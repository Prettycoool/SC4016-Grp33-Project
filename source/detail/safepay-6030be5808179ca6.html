Multico (Asia) Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based industrial equipment and engineering solutions provider. Established in 1973, the company specializes in distributing heavy machinery and providing technical support services across Southeast Asia. It offers a wide range of products including power generators, air compressors, construction equipment, and industrial engines. Multico represents renowned global brands such as Perkins, Bobcat, and Doosan. The firm supports industries like construction, marine, oil and gas, and manufacturing by offering equipment leasing, after-sales service, and spare parts. Its strategic location in Singapore allows it to serve a regional clientele with logistical efficiency. Committed to sustainability and innovation, Multico also invests in environmentally friendly technology and staff training programs to ensure service excellence and operational safety.
