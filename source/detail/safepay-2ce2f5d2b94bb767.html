KNOBEL Bau-Gruppe is a German family-owned construction company headquartered in Hartheim am Rhein, Baden-Württemberg.

Founded in 1947, the company has grown from a small sand and gravel business established after the Second World War into one of the leading regional construction firms in the border region of Germany, France, and Switzerland. Today, the business is managed by the third generation of the Knobel family and has more than 75 years of experience in civil engineering and infrastructure development. The company is recognized for combining traditional craftsmanship with modern construction technologies and sustainable business practices.
KNOBEL Bau-Gruppe provides a comprehensive portfolio of services, including road construction, civil engineering, sewer and drainage systems, earthworks, paving, road rehabilitation, demolition, asphalt milling, and environmental engineering. A distinguishing feature of the company is its vertically integrated business model. It operates its own gravel pits, asphalt and concrete production plants, recycling facilities, and earth disposal sites, enabling complete control over material production, logistics, and construction execution. This integrated approach improves efficiency, ensures consistent quality, and supports sustainable resource management through extensive recycling of construction materials.
