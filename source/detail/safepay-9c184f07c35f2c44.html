Holiday Inn Vilnius is a full-service business and leisure hotel located in central Vilnius, Lithuania, within walking distance of the city's historic Old Town.

The property operates under the international Holiday Inn brand of IHG Hotels & Resorts. The hotel provides 134 guest rooms, including standard and premium rooms and suites, and serves both business travelers and tourists visiting Lithuania's capital.
The hotel provides accommodation together with food and beverage, business and event services. Its facilities include the RIB ROOM restaurant, a lobby bar, fitness facilities, parking and several conference rooms. The meeting facilities can accommodate corporate events and meetings for up to approximately 250 people, making business events an important part of the hotel's service offering.
