Swadeshi Civil Infrastructure Private Limited (SCIPL) is an Indian private limited company incorporated on March 11, 2008, headquartered in New Delhi, India, specializing in comprehensive civil infrastructure construction projects. The company adapts international standards of business and construction to the Indian environment, with an impressive track record of over 220 completed projects delivered ahead of schedule. Core services include civil construction (roads, highways, bridges, institutional buildings); interior design and furniture; electrical and mechanical projects; billing and measurements; and design-build projects. SCIPL primarily serves government clients including CPWD (Central Public Works Department), PWD, Delhi Tourism Department, HSCC, RITES, and the President's Secretariat. Notable past projects include construction works for the Commonwealth Games 2010 Delhi.
