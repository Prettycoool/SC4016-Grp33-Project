Horst Weier GmbH Industrievertretungen is a German industrial sales agency and technical distribution company headquartered in Bocholt, North Rhine-Westphalia.

The company specializes in representing manufacturers of plumbing, heating, water treatment, pumping, drainage, and building services equipment throughout western Germany. Acting as a link between manufacturers, wholesalers, installers, and engineering firms, Horst Weier GmbH provides technical consulting, product demonstrations, sales support, and logistics services for professional customers. The company maintains warehouse facilities that enable rapid product availability and efficient distribution across its sales territory. It represents several well-known German manufacturers, including Hans Sasserath GmbH & Co. KG (SYR), a leading producer of drinking water protection and heating system technology, as well as Zehnder Pumpen GmbH, a manufacturer of pumps and wastewater systems. In addition to product sales, the company offers technical advice, project support, training for installers, and assistance with selecting appropriate engineering solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. Through decades of experience, strong technical expertise, and close cooperation with manufacturers and wholesale partners, Horst Weier GmbH has established a solid reputation as a reliable regional representative in Germany's building services and sanitary engineering industry.
