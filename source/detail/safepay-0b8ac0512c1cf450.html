Air Liquide Korea Co., Ltd. is the South Korean subsidiary of Air Liquide, a global industrial and healthcare gases group headquartered in France.

Air Liquide has operated in South Korea for several decades and supplies essential gases and related technologies to major industries, including semiconductors, electronics, steel, chemicals, manufacturing, healthcare, and energy. Its products include oxygen, nitrogen, hydrogen, argon, carbon dioxide, specialty gases, and advanced gas-management solutions used in industrial production and medical applications. Air Liquide Korea operates production facilities and pipeline networks that provide a reliable supply of industrial gases directly to major customers, particularly large semiconductor and manufacturing plants. The company also develops technologies supporting energy transition, including hydrogen and low-carbon industrial solutions. In 2026, Air Liquide completed its acquisition of DIG Airgas, a major South Korean industrial-gas producer. DIG Airgas generated approximately €510 million in revenue in 2024, employed around 550 people, and operated 60 plants and a 220-kilometre pipeline network. Following the acquisition, Air Liquide expects its combined South Korean operations to generate approximately €900 million in annual revenue and significantly expand its workforce in the country.
