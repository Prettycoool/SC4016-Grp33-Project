HBPro – Informática e Serviços, Lda. is a Portuguese information-technology and telecommunications company headquartered in Amadora, in the Lisbon metropolitan area.

Established in 1994, the company has more than three decades of experience providing technology products, infrastructure, consulting, maintenance, and technical support to business customers. Its principal registered activity is the retail trade of computers, peripheral equipment, and software, while its actual service portfolio extends considerably beyond conventional IT retail.
HBPro provides IT infrastructure design, implementation, maintenance, outsourcing, networking, cybersecurity, cloud and hosting services, data recovery, disaster recovery, Microsoft 365 solutions, and printing services. The company also specializes in corporate telecommunications, including IP telephony, unified communications, contact-center technologies, telephone systems, and SIP solutions. Its services are intended to provide businesses with an integrated technology environment and ongoing technical support.
