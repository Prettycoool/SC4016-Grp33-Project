Compact Mould Ltd. is a Canadian manufacturing company specializing in the design and production of high-precision moulds for the rigid plastic container industry.

Founded in 1978 and headquartered in Woodbridge, Ontario, the company has grown into one of North America’s major suppliers of extrusion blow moulds, stretch blow moulds, and injection stretch blow moulds. It serves manufacturers in sectors such as food and beverage packaging, household products, pharmaceuticals, and industrial containers.
The company provides complete product development services, including industrial design, 3D modeling, rapid prototyping, mould engineering, production tooling, maintenance, and repair. Compact Mould works closely with plastic packaging manufacturers to optimize container design, production efficiency, and material performance. Its operations include facilities in Canada, Mexico, and the United States, allowing it to support customers across North America.
