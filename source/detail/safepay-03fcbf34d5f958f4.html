Platinum Collision Centers is a U.S.-based auto body repair and collision restoration company operating primarily in Southern California. The business provides services such as frame straightening, paint matching, dent removal, and insurance claim handling. Known for its certified technicians and state-of-the-art facilities, Platinum Collision specializes in restoring vehicles to pre-accident condition using manufacturer-approved techniques and OEM parts. The company works closely with insurance providers and maintains a strong reputation for customer satisfaction, transparency, and quality workmanship. With multiple locations, it serves both individual car owners and commercial fleets. Platinum Collision emphasizes eco-friendly practices by using waterborne paints and recycling damaged components when possible.
