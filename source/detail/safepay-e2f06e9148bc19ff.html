The Cámara de Valencia, officially known as the Chamber of Commerce, Industry, Services and Navigation of Valencia, was founded in 1887 and serves as one of the most important business organizations in Spain. It is a public law corporation working alongside Spanish and regional authorities to promote trade, industry, and entrepreneurship in the Valencian Community. The institution provides a wide range of services, including export support, training programs, employment services, and digital transformation initiatives for small and medium enterprises. It also organizes international trade missions and offers platforms for networking between companies in Europe and beyond. With its headquarters in Valencia, the chamber represents thousands of companies, advocating for their interests and contributing to the economic competitiveness of the region.
