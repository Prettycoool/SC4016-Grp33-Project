Prado & Tuy, LLP is a boutique law firm based in California, United States, providing legal representation to individuals, businesses, and organizations across a broad range of civil litigation matters.

The firm focuses on practice areas including personal injury, wrongful death, workplace harassment, business litigation, civil settlements, and environmental litigation. With more than 30 years of combined legal experience, the firm's attorneys emphasize a collaborative approach in which legal professionals jointly evaluate each case to develop effective litigation strategies and achieve favorable outcomes for clients. Prado & Tuy, LLP is committed to delivering personalized legal services, maintaining close communication with clients throughout every stage of the legal process, from initial consultation to negotiation, mediation, trial, or settlement. The firm's attorneys combine courtroom advocacy with practical legal advice, helping clients understand their rights, assess legal risks, and make informed decisions. Serving both individual and corporate clients, Prado & Tuy, LLP has built its reputation on professional integrity, responsive client service, and thorough case preparation. Through experienced legal representation and a client-centered philosophy, the firm continues to provide comprehensive litigation and dispute resolution services throughout California.
