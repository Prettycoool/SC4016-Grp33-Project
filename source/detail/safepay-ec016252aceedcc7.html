RLC Transportes

RLC Transportes (operating under Rau Load Cargo, S.L.) is a Spanish logistics and freight-transport company based in El Puig de Santa Maria, Valencia. Founded in 1993, the company initially specialised in transporting fruits and vegetables but has since expanded into the import and export of all types of goods, with a notable focus on refrigerated transport for temperature-sensitive cargo such as food products. RLC has grown into one of Spain’s most experienced transport agencies, operating a modern fleet of over 300 trucks and maintaining multiple delegations across Spain and Italy (e.g., Valencia, Barcelona, Seville, Bologna), alongside a professional team of more than 100 logistics operators and multilingual traffic coordinators.
