MINO Group is a long-established Japanese manufacturer (founded 1946) that designs and builds screen-printing machines, stencil makers, dryers, printing accessories and specialty inks. Headquartered in Gujo City, the firm positions itself as one of Japan’s few “total” screen-printing suppliers — covering equipment manufacture, consumables and technical service — and supports both domestic customers and export markets through MINO International Ltd. Their product range serves apparel decoration, industrial printing, signage and specialty printed electronics markets. As a specialised precision-equipment manufacturer with export activity and roughly a medium-sized workforce (company profiles list ~100 employees), MINO’s sales are plausibly in the low billions of yen annually; exact audited financials were not publicly posted on the company site.
