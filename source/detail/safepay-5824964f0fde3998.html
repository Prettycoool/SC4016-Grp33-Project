Peus Münzen (Dr. Busso Peus Nachf. e.K.)

Peus Münzen, officially Dr. Busso Peus Nachfolger e.K., is one of Germany’s oldest and most prestigious coin dealers and numismatic auction houses, based in Frankfurt am Main. The firm traces its roots back to 1870, making it the oldest numismatic trading house in Germany and one of the longest-running coin auctioneers in Europe. Over more than 145 years, it has built a reputation for expertise in the trade of ancient, medieval, modern and collector coins, medals, banknotes and related numismatic items. The company operates both live and online auctions — typically held biannually — attracting international collectors and dealers. Participation in world-renowned coin fairs such as the World Money Fair in Berlin and regular presence at other major European numismatic events further extends its reach.
