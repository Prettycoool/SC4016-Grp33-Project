New Point S.p.A. is an Italian company specializing in the design, production, distribution, and wholesale supply of apparel, hosiery, underwear, and textile accessories.

Headquartered in Signa, Florence, Italy, the company was founded in 2006 and has grown into one of Italy's established suppliers serving both domestic and international retail markets. Its portfolio includes proprietary fashion brands, licensed collections, private-label manufacturing, and customized textile products for retailers, distributors, and corporate customers. In addition to wholesale distribution, New Point operates e-commerce channels and offers flexible B2B services, including dropshipping, private-label development, and tailored manufacturing solutions for fashion businesses. The company manages the entire production process, from product design and sourcing to quality control, logistics, and customer support, allowing clients to efficiently launch and expand their own product lines. New Point collaborates with retail chains, fashion stores, online merchants, and commercial partners throughout Europe while emphasizing product quality, competitive pricing, and reliable supply chain management. By combining Italian design expertise with modern manufacturing practices and integrated distribution services, the company has established itself as a trusted partner in the apparel and textile industry, providing scalable solutions for both established brands and emerging fashion businesses.
