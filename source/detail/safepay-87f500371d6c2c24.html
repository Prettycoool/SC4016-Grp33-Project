Julius Koch GmbH

Julius Koch GmbH is a historic German company founded in 1895 that specialises in the development and manufacture of high-performance technical textiles. With over 130 years of experience, it produces a wide range of specialised textile components such as cords, tapes, ladder strings, threads, nets and protective textile systems that meet demanding industry requirements. These products are widely used in sun-protection systems, industrial safety applications, agricultural protection, and other technical uses. The company operates production facilities in Germany, Poland and Ukraine, and maintains a global sales presence in countries across Europe, China and North America, serving a diverse international customer base. Julius Koch emphasises innovation, quality and efficiency in production, applying lean management methods and investing in ongoing product development, including new technical textile solutions for extreme environments. While the company is privately held and does not publish full audited financial statements, its size, export reach, and regional industrial activity suggest an annual revenue range in the low to mid-millions of euros.
