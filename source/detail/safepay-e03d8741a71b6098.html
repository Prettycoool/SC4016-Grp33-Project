Fe Del Mundo Medical Center (FDMMC) is a private multi-specialty hospital in Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines, providing healthcare services to both children and adults.

The institution traces its origins to 1957, when Filipino pediatrician and scientist Dr. Fe del Mundo established Children's Memorial Hospital on Banawe Street. The original facility had a capacity of 107 beds and primarily served pediatric patients. It subsequently developed into the broader medical center operating today.
The hospital provides a comprehensive range of medical services, including pediatrics, primary care, adult medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, surgery, emergency medicine, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, and hemodialysis. Its emergency department operates 24 hours a day, while specialized departments provide diagnostic and treatment services for patients with different medical conditions.
