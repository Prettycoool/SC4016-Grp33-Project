B.R.S. Cappuccio S.r.l. is an Italian company operating in the healthcare and medical supplies sector.

Headquartered in Casandrino, near Naples in the Campania region, the company specializes in the distribution and supply of medical devices, healthcare equipment, orthopedic products, and related medical support solutions. The company serves hospitals, healthcare facilities, clinics, pharmacies, and private customers, providing products designed to support patient care and rehabilitation.
Its activities are focused on supplying high-quality medical and sanitary products in compliance with Italian and European healthcare regulations. Companies in this sector typically provide mobility aids, orthopedic supports, diagnostic instruments, rehabilitation devices, and consumable medical supplies. Through its operations, B.R.S. Cappuccio contributes to the healthcare supply chain and supports medical professionals and patients requiring specialized equipment. The company emphasizes reliability, product quality, and customer service, which are essential characteristics in the healthcare industry.
