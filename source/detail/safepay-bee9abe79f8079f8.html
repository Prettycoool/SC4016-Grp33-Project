GSN Gestión y Administración de Fincas S.L. is a Spanish company specializing in property management and administration of residential and commercial real estate.

The company is based in Villaviciosa de Odón, Madrid, and operates through the GSN Gestión brand. Its website states that the organization has more than 20 years of experience in property advisory and administration, although the current legal entity was incorporated in December 2024. Its principal business activity is the professional management of homeowners' associations and properties.
GSN Gestión provides a broad range of administrative, financial, and operational services for residential communities, shared-property developments, industrial estates, garages, and other managed properties. Services include preparation of annual budgets, calculation and collection of community fees, accounting, financial reporting, management of unpaid fees, preparation of meeting documentation and minutes, and representation before public authorities. The company also provides assistance with contracts, employee administration, insurance, maintenance, and supervision of building works.
