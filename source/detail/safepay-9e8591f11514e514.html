Springer’s Jewelers

Springer’s Jewelers is a historic family-owned jewelry retailer based in Portland, Maine, with additional store locations in Bath, Maine and Portsmouth, New Hampshire. Established in 1870, it is one of the oldest continuously operating jewelers in New England, serving multiple generations of customers with fine jewelry, engagement and wedding rings, estate and pre-owned pieces, watches, custom designs, and jewelry repair services. The company positions itself as a full-service jeweler celebrating life’s special moments, offering not only new products from leading designers and luxury brands but also expert appraisals, repairs and custom craftsmanship. The business maintains a strong community presence and generational legacy, with leadership and staff who emphasize personalised service and long-term customer relationships built on quality and trust.
