Century Maritime Agencies, Inc. (CENMAR) is a Philippine manning and crew-recruitment agency specializing in the recruitment, selection, training, and deployment of Filipino seafarers for international maritime employment.

The company was registered with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission in 1997 and received authorization to operate as a manning agency in 1998. Its principal activity is supplying qualified Filipino crew members—including officers and ratings—to international shipowners and vessel operators.

CENMAR's services cover the recruitment and screening of seafarers, verification of professional qualifications and certificates, medical fitness requirements, training coordination, and deployment of personnel according to the specific requirements of vessel owners and operators. The company maintains a pool of experienced maritime professionals and aims to provide appropriately qualified crew for different types of vessels and positions. Its operations are conducted within the regulatory framework governing overseas Filipino workers and maritime manning agencies in the Philippines.
