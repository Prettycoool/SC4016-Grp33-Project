Recovery Café is a U.S. nonprofit organization headquartered in Seattle, Washington, dedicated to providing long-term recovery support for people affected by trauma, homelessness, addiction, and mental-health challenges.

The organization was founded in 2003 and opened its first physical café in Seattle in 2004. Its approach is based on the principle that sustained recovery requires not only treatment, but also stable relationships, community, belonging, and access to practical social services. Recovery Café provides a safe, drug- and alcohol-free environment where members can receive free meals, coffee, peer support, educational programs, referrals, and opportunities for personal development. Its core programs include Recovery Circles, which provide structured peer-to-peer support, and the School for Recovery, which offers classes addressing addiction, coping skills, mental health, and personal development. The organization also facilitates access to healthcare, housing assistance, employment resources, legal services, and substance-use treatment through partnerships with external providers. Recovery Café operates locations in Seattle and has developed the Recovery Café Network, which supports communities seeking to replicate its recovery-oriented model. The organization is funded primarily through charitable contributions, program revenue, and other nonprofit funding sources. Its work has made it a notable example of a community-based, recovery-oriented social-service model in the United States.
