POL-HUN

POL-HUN is a well-established Polish manufacturer and distributor of household cleaning products and consumer chemicals. Founded in 1990, the company has grown from a small venture into one of Poland’s leading producers of a wide range of cleaning agents, air fresheners, detergents, specialised household cleaners, sponges, wipes, packaging and private-label products for domestic and international markets. POL-HUN’s manufacturing operations are based in Koluszki in the Łódź Voivodeship, where it maintains a modern production complex with extensive machinery capable of handling synthesis, formulation, packaging and distribution. The company specialises in developing its own branded products under names such as General Fresh — particularly known for toilet rim blocks — while also providing contract manufacturing and private-label services for retailers and international clients. POL-HUN’s product portfolio includes household surface cleaners, dishwashing liquids, drain openers, multi-purpose agents, car air fresheners and more, reflecting a broad market reach. Over more than three decades, the firm has expanded its logistics, production and R&D capabilities, and today counts hundreds of employees among its workforce, making it a major local employer in Koluszki.
