Tavola S.p.A. is an Italian consumer goods company headquartered in Milan and founded in 1947.

Over more than seventy years of operation, the company has evolved from an import-export business into a diversified manufacturer, distributor, and brand owner in the fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) sector. The company operates across three major product categories: personal care, home care, and automotive care. Its portfolio includes both proprietary brands and internationally licensed products distributed through supermarkets, drugstores, DIY retail chains, automotive accessory stores, and fuel stations.
Tavola is particularly known for managing well-established brands such as Arbre Magique®, Bullock®, Podovis®, Mister Magic®, Orphea®, Care For You®, and Cerox®. Throughout its history, the company expanded through acquisitions, exclusive distribution agreements, and product innovation, strengthening its position in several consumer product categories. The company also operates internationally through Tavola Iberica in Portugal and Spain and maintains distribution partnerships in markets including the United States, China, Mexico, and Australia.
