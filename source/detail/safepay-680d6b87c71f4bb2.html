SORARIS S.p.A. is an Italian environmental services company headquartered in Sandrigo, in the Province of Vicenza, Veneto.

Founded in 1983, the company specializes in the collection, transportation, treatment, and management of municipal solid waste and recyclable materials. It operates as a publicly owned company whose shareholders are 18 municipalities in the Vicenza area, providing waste management services to approximately 125,000 residents and thousands of commercial users.
The company's core activities include household waste collection, recycling programs, environmental sanitation services, operation of waste transfer facilities, and management of local waste tariffs. SORARIS has gained recognition in Italy for its high recycling rates, innovation in waste management, and implementation of digital systems for tracking waste collection and improving service efficiency. The organization has also developed projects focused on environmental sustainability, circular economy principles, and reducing landfill disposal through increased material recovery.
