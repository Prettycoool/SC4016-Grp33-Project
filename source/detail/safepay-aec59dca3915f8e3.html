Eagle Crest Communities is a nonprofit senior healthcare organization headquartered in La Crosse, Wisconsin.

Operating under Bethany Lutheran Homes, Inc., the organization has served the region since 1946 and is recognized as the largest nonprofit provider of senior living and long-term care services in western Wisconsin. Over the past two decades, Eagle Crest has invested more than USD 100 million in developing modern senior living facilities and healthcare services. The organization operates multiple communities in La Crosse, Onalaska, and Holmen, providing a complete continuum of care that includes independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing, rehabilitation, and transitional care.
The organization's mission is centered on providing compassionate, faith-based care while promoting dignity, independence, and quality of life for older adults. In addition to residential care, Eagle Crest offers wellness programs, physical and occupational rehabilitation, social activities, transportation services, nutritional support, and specialized memory care for individuals living with dementia. The organization continually invests in employee education, technology, and facility modernization to improve healthcare outcomes and resident satisfaction. Recent reports indicate a workforce of approximately 550 employees, making it one of the region's largest healthcare employers.
