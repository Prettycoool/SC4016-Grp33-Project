The Wirtschaftsverband der deutschen Kautschukindustrie e.V. (WDK) is the principal trade association representing Germany's rubber and tire manufacturing industry.

Founded in 1950 and headquartered in Frankfurt am Main, the organization serves as the central voice of German manufacturers of tires, technical elastomer products, and other rubber-based industrial components. The association represents more than 200 member companies, making it one of the most influential industrial organizations within Germany's automotive and materials manufacturing sectors.
WDK's primary mission is to advocate for the interests of the German rubber industry at national and European levels. The association works on regulatory affairs, environmental policy, sustainability initiatives, recycling programs, technical standards, international trade issues, and industrial competitiveness. It maintains close cooperation with government agencies, European institutions, research organizations, and international industry groups. WDK is also active in areas such as tire technology, circular economy development, climate policy, raw-material supply chains, and industrial innovation.
