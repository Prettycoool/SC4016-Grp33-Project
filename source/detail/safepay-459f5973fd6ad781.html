Gob.pe is the Plataforma Digital Única del Estado Peruano, the official unified digital platform of the Government of Peru.

It serves as the country's primary online point of contact between public institutions and citizens, providing government information, administrative procedures, digital services, regulations, publications, campaigns and institutional news. The platform is administered by the Presidencia del Consejo de Ministros (PCM) through the Secretariat of Government and Digital Transformation, while individual government institutions remain responsible for the content published on their respective pages.
The platform consolidates information from ministries, regional governments, municipalities and other public-sector institutions. Citizens can use Gob.pe to access services such as identification-related procedures, tax and administrative services, healthcare information, certificates, permits, public-sector recruitment information and government publications.
