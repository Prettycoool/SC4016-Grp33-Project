PAUAT Architekten

PAUAT Architekten is a regional architectural practice based in Wels, Austria, founded in 1999 and led by architect Heinz Plöderl. The firm focuses on energy-efficient, resource-conscious and sustainable architecture, blending ecological construction principles with functional design across a variety of project types — from private homes and residential buildings to industrial sites, public facilities and urban planning assignments. Its work emphasises thoughtful integration of structures into their context and life-cycle-oriented building practices, including passive and low-energy construction approaches that reflect broader European environmental standards. PAUAT has completed notable projects such as revitalisation work and exhibition halls, demonstrating its ability to handle complex architectural challenges while pursuing efficiency and sustainability goals.
