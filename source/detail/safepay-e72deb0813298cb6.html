Envases Group

Envases Group is a privately held multinational packaging manufacturer and solutions provider with deep roots in the global containers and packaging industry. The company specialises in aluminium packaging, particularly for beauty, cosmetics, fragrance, pharmaceutical, personal care and luxury consumer products. Over more than 75 years in business, Envases has developed proprietary technologies and manufacturing expertise, producing billions of aluminium cans, bottles and custom packaging solutions sold under private-label and B2B arrangements to well-known brands around the world. The company operates two modern production plants in Europe, enabling tailored design, tooling and production capabilities that support client innovation and responsiveness. Envases also maintains a widespread global presence supported by international distributors and representatives across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Oceania, allowing it to serve customers in over 30 countries.
