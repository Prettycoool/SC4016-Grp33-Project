TRIWO Hahn Airport GmbH is the operator of Hahn Airport (HHN), an international commercial airport located in Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany.

Originally developed as a United States military air base, the facility began civilian operations in 1993 and has since become one of Germany's most important regional airports. Since May 2023, the airport has been owned by TRIWO AG, which has invested in infrastructure modernization and operational improvements. Hahn Airport operates 24 hours a day, making it one of the few German airports without night-flight restrictions, a significant advantage for cargo transportation and express logistics.
The airport's business model is built on two major pillars: passenger aviation and air freight. In 2024, Hahn Airport handled approximately 1.87 million passengers and processed more than 105,000 tonnes of cargo, making it one of Germany's leading cargo airports. The airport serves over 30 international destinations, with Ryanair operating the majority of scheduled passenger flights. Its strategic location within the European economic corridor known as the "Blue Banana" provides direct access to major industrial and commercial markets across Germany, France, Belgium, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands.
