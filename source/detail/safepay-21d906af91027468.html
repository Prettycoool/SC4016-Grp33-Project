TransElectric, part of the Israeli Trans Innovation Group (TIG), is a value-add distributor of electronic components and electro-mechanical solutions. Based in the Hevel Modi’in Industrial Park, TransElectric represents global brands such as 3M, Phoenix Contact, ODU, Belden, and Fujitsu. The company offers tailored supply-chain models, design support, manufacturing, and distribution services—including custom cable assemblies and harnesses compliant with ISO and AS9100 standards. With engineering-backed solutions catering to telecom, medical, military, and automation markets, TransElectric serves demanding international customer needs.
